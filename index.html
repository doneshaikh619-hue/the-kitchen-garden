OUR HERITAGE & PHILOSOPHY

A Culinary Haven in the Heart of Diamond City

Established at the scenic Country Club along Chaprar Road, Sialkot, The Kitchen Garden was born out of a passion to combine exceptional gourmet gastronomy with the serene, restorative beauty of nature.

While diners in Sialkot frequently frequented crowded cantt restaurants, The Kitchen Garden introduced a peaceful, expansive sanctuary with lush manicured lawns, romantic fairy lights, and an expansive multi-cuisine menu covering traditional BBQ, authentic karahis, gourmet steaks, and pan-Asian favorites.
